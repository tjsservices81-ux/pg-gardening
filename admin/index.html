If the login box does not appear

It is one of two switches in the Netlify dashboard, and both are one-off:

  1. Site configuration → Identity → Enable Identity
  2. Identity → Services → Git Gateway → Enable

If it will not finish loading

  • Open it in Safari or Chrome properly. If you came here by tapping a link inside Gmail, Facebook or Messages, you are in a cut-down browser those apps run themselves, and it handles big downloads badly. Tap the ⋯ or share button and choose Open in Safari / Open in Chrome.
  • Get on wi-fi if you can. It only has to download once — after that it is cached.
  • Pull down to refresh and give it another go. A half-finished download does not resume; a fresh one usually works.

If you clicked an invite email and landed on the ordinary website

That is Netlify sending its invites to the front page rather than here, and the site now forwards you across automatically. If it happened before that fix went up, open the email again and click the link a second time — or paste the whole address in with /admin/ before the #invite_token=… part.
